Hexation

What Is Hexation?

Hexation is the next level after pentation: towers of towers of towers! Even tiny inputs create numbers so huge they are hard to imagine. Scientists use these ideas to study very fast growth.

2 ↑↑↑↑ 2 = 2 ↑↑↑ 2 = 4
2 ↑↑↑↑ 3 = 2 ↑↑↑ (2 ↑↑↑ 2) = 2 ↑↑↑ 4 (already enormous)
