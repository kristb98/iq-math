Powers

What Are Powers?

A power is a shortcut for repeated multiplication. The base is the number being multiplied, and the exponent tells how many times to multiply it.

23 means "multiply 2 by itself 3 times": 2 × 2 × 2 = 8.

23 = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8
32 = 3 × 3 = 9
53 = 5 × 5 × 5 = 125
104 = 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 = 10000
