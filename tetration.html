Tetration

What Is Tetration?

Tetration is "powers stacked on top of powers." If powers are repeated multiplication, tetration is repeated exponentiation.

32 means a tower of three 2s: 2(22) = 24 = 16.

22 = 22 = 4
32 = 2(22) = 24 = 16
23 = 33 = 27
33 = 3(33) = 327 = 7625597484987
