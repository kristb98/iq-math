Physics & Dimensions / E=mc²

Einstein's Most Famous Equation

E = mc²

This equation tells us that mass and energy are interchangeable.

  • E = energy
  • m = mass
  • c = speed of light in a vacuum (≈ 299,792,458 m/s)

Because c² is enormous, even a tiny amount of mass can be converted into a huge amount of energy.

Example

If m = 1 kg, then:

E = 1 × (299,792,458)² ≈ 8.99 × 1016 joules
