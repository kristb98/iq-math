Beltmatic: Factorial Factory Guide

1. Drawing Belts & Moving Around

• Left-Click & Drag on empty tiles to draw auto-curving Conveyor Belts!

• Right-Click (or use Erase) to delete a belt or machine. Press R to rotate before placing.

• Pan & Zoom: Drag with middle-mouse or WASD/Arrow keys to move around the grid; scroll wheel or + / − to zoom.

2. How Machines Expand Factorials

• Unpacker (n!): Place directly on or after a number! When 4 enters, it outputs 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 one by one! For 0, it outputs 1 (0! = 1).

• Splitter (⑂): Alternates incoming numbers left & right — perfect for sending an Unpacker stream into both sides of a Multiplier!

• Multiplier (×): Takes 2 side inputs (blue arrows) and outputs their product (A × B) out the front!

• Minus 1 (-1): Subtracts 1 from a number (4 → 3) so you can build custom recursive factorial chains!
