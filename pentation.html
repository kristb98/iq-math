Pentation

What Is Pentation?

Pentation is "tetration stacked on top of tetration." It makes towers of towers! These numbers become gigantic very quickly, so we usually just look at small examples.

2 ↑↑↑ 2 = 22 = 4
2 ↑↑↑ 3 = 42 = 2(2(22)) = 65536
